🎯 What This Mod Does

AI Influence is a revolutionary total conversion mod that fundamentally transforms Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord by adding sophisticated artificial intelligence systems to manage virtually every aspect of the game world. It turns static NPCs into living, breathing characters with memory, personality, and agency while introducing dynamic world events, intelligent diplomacy, and complex relationship systems.

Core Concept: The mod replaces scripted interactions with real-time AI-generated content, making every playthrough unique and responsive to your actions. No two conversations, diplomatic events, or world developments are ever the same.

v4.1.0 (March 2026)

New: Group conversation — talk to several NPCs at once during missions (towns, keeps, lord halls, etc.). Press Alt+T near lords, clan companions, wanderers, or notables within ~5 m: your line is broadcast to everyone in range, and each hears the others’ replies in the same round (shared group history in their AI context). Participants update as people move: new arrivals can join, those who walk away are paused until they return within a larger grace radius — you end the talk when you leave, not automatically. With TTS on, responses are prepared in a pipeline (next NPC while the current line plays). If nearby NPCs were in a private scripted exchange, your message can interrupt and pull them into the group. Requires the main mod toggle; works like the rest of AI dialog (API, prompts, technical actions where applicable).
Battle Tactics AI — LLM-driven orders in field battles (not sieges). The battle leader on each side (player faction general, or an AI lord if you are not the general) periodically gets a tactical snapshot of formations, casualties, and combat status, then issues real in-game orders via the native order system. Optional: Alt+B natural-language commands for your side (interpreted by AI and converted to orders). Optional formation captains (companions or enemy heroes) command only their formation. If your commander falls, the next best tactician on the team takes over. Commander shouts and troop barks can show as on-screen phrases and in the log; battle chatter can optionally be written into lords’ histories for Dynamic Events. All options live under MCM «Battle Tactics AI» (master switch, interval, player text commands, ally/enemy captain AI, log/display, dynamic events logging).
Fixed: Diplomacy: fixed diplomatic / world events vanishing from the interface when they should stay visible (statements and event entries no longer disappear incorrectly).
Settlement transfer in dialogue: fixed handing over or receiving fiefs through NPC conversation — transfers that should execute after agreement in chat now resolve correctly instead of failing silently or losing the settlement.
Bug fixes and improvements.
